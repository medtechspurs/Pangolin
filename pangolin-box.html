Pangolin in a box

v2.4

Your phone is the open end of a foot-deep box. Tip and turn the phone and the box goes with it: whatever wall is lowest becomes the floor. Flick the phone to bounce things.

Lay boulders, knock out the barriers and robots, then curl up and roll into the cannon. Turn the phone to aim the far window at a saucer, hold Launch to charge, and let go. One shot per level.

On a desktop, use arrow keys or drag.
